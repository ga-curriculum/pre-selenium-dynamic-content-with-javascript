## Dynamic Content and Its Impact on Automation

### Delivery tips

- Encourage learners to share their personal observations with global websites they use, broadening relevance beyond any single region or brand.
- Highlight the connection between what learners see in DevTools and how Selenium “sees” the page.
- Avoid jargon unless clearly defined. When introducing terms like _DOM_ or _AJAX_, use both definitions and analogies.
- If running the activity live, prompt learners to use sites with diverse, dynamic features to maximize their exposure to a variety of scenarios.

---

## Asynchronous Content Loading Patterns

### Delivery tips

- Encourage learners to use websites that reflect diverse international audiences and real-world use cases.
- Ask open-ended questions during the activity to prompt observation and analysis, not just reporting steps.
- Remind learners that asynchronous loading can appear in many visual forms—not just spinners, but also skeletons, placeholders, or brief transitions.
- If teaching live or remotely, facilitate short pair or group sharing after the activity to help learners compare findings and deepen their understanding.

### Knowledge check answers

1. **Answer:** B) A loading spinner or skeleton screen appears before new content is displayed.
2. **Answer:** B) XHR and Fetch requests
